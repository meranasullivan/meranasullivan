# Merana Sullivan

I work where business development, local growth and economic storytelling meet. Based in Paris, France, I help turn broad commercial ambitions into practical questions, useful introductions and clear next steps.

My interests include business promotion, development consulting and economic journalism. I am especially curious about the small decisions that shape a neighbourhood: how a shop finds its customers, what makes a partnership useful, and who benefits when an area changes.

I value careful research, plain language and conversations with the people doing the work. I use this space to organise notes and explore ideas that connect evidence with everyday business decisions.
